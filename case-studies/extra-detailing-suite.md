# Extra Detailing Suite

- ExtraDetailingTools adds a transform gizmo, snap-to-surface toggle, and curated asset menus.
- ExtraAssetsImporter loads unofficial surface and decal packs with best-practice messaging until the official editor ships.
- ExtraLib centralises shared systems, UI, and translation support for the Extra toolchain.
- **Takeaway:** when shipping a family of detailing mods, isolate shared libraries, pair UX affordances with curated content, and document editor parity.
