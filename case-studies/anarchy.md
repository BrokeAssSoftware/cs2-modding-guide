# Anarchy

- Suppresses placement error checks (overlap, shoreline, lot limits) on demand and surfaces a toggleable tool icon plus shortcut.
- Adds elevation lock and relative elevation controls for props, trees, and nets; supports per-tool auto toggles and optional unique-building duplication.
- Provides compatibility switches such as auto-disabling while brushing and refreshing culled props.
- **Takeaway:** adopt this approach for opt-in sandbox affordances with granular safety rails and per-tool behaviour.
